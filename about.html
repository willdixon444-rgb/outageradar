About

OutageRadar is run by Ghost Drive, a builder who runs several trading bots without being a professional programmer.

Every line of code behind my bots was written with AI. I describe what I want, test what comes back, and keep going until it works. Along the way I’ve tried a lot of tools, wasted money on some, and found a setup that works for me.

This site shares that setup honestly. I don’t sell signals, I don’t make performance claims, and I only write about tools I’ve used myself.

I started with no programming background, just an interest in trading and a lot of late nights. Today I run several bots: a momentum scanner for day trading and bots that trade prediction markets on Kalshi, all hosted in the cloud with alerts sent straight to my phone. None of them is a finished success story. I’m still testing, measuring and improving, and this site is where I share the tools and lessons along the way.
