Privacy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

OutageRadar has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking. We count page views and link clicks in aggregate (date, page, country and the website you came from). We do not store IP addresses or use cookies or IDs, so the counts can’t identify you. We don’t sell or share data.

What happens when you visit

  • Website hosting. The site is hosted by Render, which keeps standard server logs (such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested) as part of running the service. See Render’s privacy policy.
  • Live status data. The board loads its status data, and sends the visit counts below, through our own address, data.liveoutageradar.com, which runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare keeps short-lived technical logs of these requests (such as the time, the address requested and network details, which can include your IP address) for a few days. We use them only for troubleshooting. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Visit counts. Each page sends one small message to data.liveoutageradar.com when it loads, and one when you click a link to another website. It contains only the page, the domain of the website that sent you here (for example google.com, never the full address), a campaign tag if the link had one (such as utm_source=bluesky), and whether your browser reports being automated. Cloudflare adds your country. We add one to that day’s total and keep only the totals, for about 13 months. We do not store IP addresses, browser details or any ID, and nothing is saved on your device.
  • Your browser stores nothing from us: no cookies and no local storage.

Links to other sites

Each service name on the board links to that provider’s official status page, and we link to TokenMargin. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Email

If you email contact@liveoutageradar.com, your message passes through Cloudflare’s email forwarding to our inbox. We use it only to reply to you.

Changes

If we add ads or affiliate links, or change what we count, we will update this page before we do, and add a notice where required.
