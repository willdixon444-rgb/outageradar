What it means

Many developers still see the message “You exceeded your current quota” or the type insufficient_quota. OpenAI’s current docs split this into specific causes, all with status 429. Check error.code to see which one you have; the broader error.type can still be insufficient_quota.

  • credit_balance_exhausted: Your organization has no prepaid credits left. Add credits in your billing settings.
  • organization_spend_limit_exceeded: Your organization reached the monthly spend limit it set. It applies to every project in the organization. Raise or remove it in your organization limits, or wait for the monthly reset.
  • project_spend_limit_exceeded: One project reached its own monthly spend limit. Other projects can keep working. Raise or remove it in the project’s settings, or wait for the monthly reset.
  • organization_usage_limit_exceeded: Your organization reached the monthly usage limit OpenAI assigned to it, which is separate from the limits you set. Request a higher approved usage limit in your organization limits, or contact OpenAI support.

What to do

  1. Find error.code in the response and follow the matching fix above.
  2. Retrying will not restore access. OpenAI says to update the credits or limits first, then send the request again.
